---

## Clear Space

Maintain a minimum clear space equal to half the mark's height on all sides.
Nothing — type, edges, other logos — should enter this zone.

---

## Minimum Sizes

- Digital: 24px minimum
- Favicon: 16px (aperture may close — acceptable)
- Embroidery / pins: 1.5 inch minimum

---

## Do

- Use approved palette only
- Use mono variant on busy or photographic backgrounds
- Respect clear space at all sizes

## Do Not

- Recolor the mark off-brand
- Add glow, shadow, or 3D effects
- Stretch, skew, or rotate the mark
- Use violet as the wordmark color (reserved for aperture/accents)

---

## Pin / Merch Vendor Instructions

For enamel pins, send: ARIA_pin_mark_only.svg or ARIA_pin_wordmark.svg
- Hard enamel colors: Navy #0B0E28, White #FFFFFF, Violet #6D3AF2
- Minimum pin diameter: 1 inch
- The outer ring uses violet as the metal line color

For embroidery (polos, hoodies, hats):
- Send ARIA_mark_mono_white.svg (on dark fabric) or ARIA_mark_mono_navy.svg (on light fabric)
- Minimum embroidery size: 1.5 inches
- Single thread color only — do not attempt two-tone at small scale
